# AGENT-002 Marketplace Positioning

## Positioning statement

Skill ROI Agent is a transparent decision system for choosing skills that
compound a creator's capability, portfolio, and earning opportunities.

## Target customers

- Freelancers choosing profitable specializations
- Creators planning products or services
- Career switchers comparing learning paths
- Small teams prioritizing capability development

## Product promise

Replace vague “learn this skill” advice with an evidence-backed scorecard,
small validation experiments, and a reviewable roadmap.

## Packaging

| Edition | Included capability | Audience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | Baseline, scorecard, and next-step plan | Individual learners |
| Professional | Market evidence, scenarios, and experiments | Freelancers and creators |
| Team | Shared capability planning and review cycles | Small teams |

## Marketplace requirements

- Sample scorecard using non-sensitive data
- Evidence and assumptions clearly labeled
- Setup guide and scoring glossary
- Demonstration from goal intake to roadmap refresh
