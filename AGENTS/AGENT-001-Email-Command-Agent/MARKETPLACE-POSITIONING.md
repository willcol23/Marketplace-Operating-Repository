# AGENT-001 Marketplace Positioning

## Positioning statement

Email Command Agent is an approval-first email operations assistant that turns
an overloaded inbox into a prioritized, executable command queue.

## Target customers

- Independent consultants and freelancers
- Small agencies and creator-led businesses
- Founders managing sales, support, and partnerships
- Operations coordinators with recurring email workloads

## Customer problem

Important requests, commitments, and follow-ups are buried across threads.
Existing automation often acts without enough context or approval.

## Differentiators

- Human approval gates for consequential actions
- Source-linked summaries and audit trails
- Command-oriented workflows instead of one-off prompts
- Extensible handoffs to calendars, task systems, and CRMs

## Packaging

| Edition | Included capability | Suggested audience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | Triage, summaries, action extraction, daily digest | Individual creators |
| Professional | Drafts, follow-ups, calendar coordination, task handoff | Freelancers and consultants |
| Team | Shared inbox routing, escalation rules, analytics | Small teams and agencies |

## Marketplace listing requirements

- Five-minute setup guide
- Demonstration inbox with safe, non-sensitive sample messages
- Screenshots of queue, approval, and audit views
- Short demo covering triage through approved follow-up
- Clear integration and permission requirements
