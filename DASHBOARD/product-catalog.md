## Scoring guidance

- **Revenue:** Expected ability to support direct or recurring revenue.
- **Complexity:** Integration, safety, documentation, and maintenance effort.
- **Demand:** Strength and breadth of the customer problem.
- **Marketplace value:** Reusability, differentiation, bundle fit, and creator appeal.
- **Implementation status:** Planned, Defined, Validated, Packaged, or Released.

Scores are directional and should be recalculated when evidence, scope, or
market conditions change.
